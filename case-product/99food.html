01 — Overview

The
Brief

At 99Food, my work was to turn acquisition needs into design hypotheses, modular systems, and experiments that helped restaurant owners understand value, build trust, and advance through the funnel.

The audience wasn't a typical consumer — it was a business owner deciding whether to trust a new platform with their livelihood. Every design decision had to answer: what's in it for my business?

Responsibilities
  • Design hypotheses & experimentation strategy
  • Quarterly experimentation roadmap
  • Performance analysis & biweekly reporting
  • Modular creative system design
  • Fake refresh methodology

02 — Context

The Product Behind
the Campaigns

The challenge wasn't just writing prettier ads. 99Food needed to attract restaurant partners at scale — with clear messaging for different audiences, in different regions, at different moments of the funnel. The design work had to connect business, behavior, and conversion.

User

Restaurant owner seeking predictability, demand, and trust.

Business

Grow the partner base with efficiency.

Channel

Digital campaigns and acquisition touchpoints.

Complexity

Multiple audiences, multiple regions, little attention span.

03 — Root Causes

Why This Was a
Product Problem

The funnel revealed opportunities before the final conversion — not just at the sign-up screen, but at every step a restaurant owner had to trust before getting there.

  • Unclear Value Proposition

    The benefit wasn't always obvious at a glance.

  • Low Initial Trust

    Credibility signals were missing at the right moment.

  • High Variation, Low System

    Learnings didn't scale with consistency.

  • Audiences With Different Objections

    One message didn't serve everyone.

Guided the Work

Every design decision started from a testable belief about restaurant-owner behavior — tracked, validated, and reused across campaigns.

Test, Refresh & Scale

Core of the acquisition funnel. The "High Demand + Guaranteed Delivery" angle represented ~74% of total national impressions in peak periods.

Video Ads

Short-form with strong hooks. Consistently outperformed static in CTR — +0.55 p.p. above campaign average.

UGC Concepts

Creator-style content to build peer trust with restaurant owners.

Localized Versions

City-specific adaptations for the 12-city expansion — no full production per market.

07 — Testing the System

Experiments That
Moved the Funnel

Three experiment tracks tested the system's core hypotheses — messaging, creative refresh, and regional relevance — each validated against real performance signals.

A · Finding the Angle

Messaging

Structured copystorming built messages from the restaurant owner's real pains and motivations. Six directions tested simultaneously.

~74% of impressions — "High Demand" angleGeneric copy = baseline

B · Fake Refresh Strategy

Creative Refresh

Lightweight visual and copy updates on top-performing ads extended delivery without resetting platform learning.

+22% reach-9% CPC260% clicks vs. others

C · One Country, Many Audiences

Regional

Context and profile adjustments balanced consistency and relevance across urban, interior, new, and experienced audiences.

6 cities analyzedDistinct triggers per market

The bottom line: With only +6% more spend, the right creative approach delivered +22% reach, +17% clicks, and +14% engagement — with lower CPM and CPC across the board.

08 — Campaign Highlights

The Moments That
Made the Funnel Move

Seasonal and performance campaigns consistently outperformed evergreen assets — combining timing, relevance, and a clear business value proposition for the restaurant owner.

Easter Campaign

Q1 · National · Meta

Launched with "sell more at Easter with zero monthly fee" messaging. Migrated to fake refresh mid-campaign, extending performance and stabilizing cost efficiency through April.

23% of total sign-ups−11% vs account avg CPSUTop creative of the period

Consumer Month

Q1 · National · Meta

Combined consumer demand momentum with zero monthly fee offer. Fake refresh applied on day 17 — switched from static to template format, stabilizing performance through month-end.

AI Bag Delivery
