03 — The Challenge

Four Constraints,
One Direction

Scaling B2B acquisition through paid media comes with compounding constraints. The environment demanded creative efficiency above all — less budget waste, more signal, faster iteration.

Rising CPC

A +27% increase in market CPC during Q1 made creative efficiency the primary lever for protecting acquisition costs.

Creative Fatigue

High-frequency campaigns burned through assets fast. Without a refresh strategy, performance would plateau and costs would spike.

Regional Complexity

Expanding to 12 new cities meant localized messaging — but production couldn't scale 1:1 with geography.

B2B Specificity

Restaurant owners don't respond to consumer logic. Every angle had to connect to real business value — not just visual appeal.

07 — Key Strategy

Fake Refresh
Strategy

The strategy emerged from a pattern observed through Smartly.io's creative feed: by monitoring performance changes across creatives published simultaneously via the platform's live feed, it became clear that lightweight visual updates on top-performing ads were extending delivery without triggering a new learning phase.

Instead of rebuilding ads from scratch — which resets platform learning, spikes CPC, and loses accumulated delivery efficiency — Fake Refresh applied BG swaps and copy angle updates to preserve the ad's identity while giving the algorithm a renewed signal.

09 — Regional

One Country,
Many Audiences

National campaigns set the base. Regional data revealed distinct conversion triggers per city — shaping creative adaptation for the 12-city expansion. Instagram Feed and Stories led across all markets.

São Paulo

Seasonal creatives drove the highest engagement. Easter "Chocolate" asset secured #1 position in the period.

Rio de Janeiro

Consumer Month had the strongest traction. Lifestyle-oriented messaging aligned better with local culture than ROI angles.

Manaus

Entrepreneurship + seasonal combination outperformed. Video format led both CPC and engagement throughout the period.

Brasília

Only market where static had lower CPC than video — a unique audience signal that informed creative allocation for this city.

Fortaleza

National creative consistently outperformed city-specific versions. Running the national ad was the optimal strategy for this market.

Porto Alegre

ROI-focused messaging resonated with the entrepreneurial profile. Video led CPC efficiency with the lowest cost per engagement.
