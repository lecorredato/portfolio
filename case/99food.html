03 — The Challenge

Scaling
Performance

Maintain acquisition performance at scale while combating creative fatigue in a highly competitive paid media environment.

The operation required scalable creative production, faster experimentation, seasonal responsiveness, localized communication and performance consistency across multiple regions.

Rising CPC

Increased competition drove up costs, making creative efficiency critical to maintaining ROI.

Creative Fatigue

High-frequency campaigns accelerated ad fatigue, requiring continuous creative refreshes.

Scaling Complexity

Expanding to new cities demanded localized content without proportionally scaling production.

Regional Differences

Each market responded differently — a single national creative wasn't enough.

08 — Regional

Regional
Performance Insights

Different cities responded differently to creative approaches, proving the importance of localized communication and adaptive messaging.

São Paulo

Seasonal creatives drove higher engagement. National campaigns performed well when paired with city-specific offers.

Manaus

Launch-focused communication performed better. Urgency and exclusivity messaging resonated stronger in this market.

Fortaleza

Trust-driven messaging improved conversion quality. Proof-based creatives outperformed aspirational angles.

Brasília

Localized creatives outperformed national versions. Regional identity in visuals increased relevance significantly.

Rio de Janeiro

Consumer month campaigns had stronger traction. Lifestyle-oriented messaging aligned with local culture.

Porto Alegre

Business-value messaging drove sign-ups. ROI-focused copy resonated with the entrepreneurial market profile.
