02 — Overview

The
Brief

In January 2026, I joined 99Food as a Creative Strategist with one clear mandate: grow the restaurant partner base through B2B acquisition campaigns on Meta and Google.

03 — The Challenge

Three Problems,
One System

Scaling B2B acquisition through paid media comes with compounding constraints. The environment demanded creative efficiency above all else — less budget waste, more learning signal, faster iteration.

Rising CPC

A +27% increase in overall market CPC during Q1 made creative efficiency the primary lever for protecting acquisition costs.

Creative Fatigue

High-frequency campaigns burned through assets fast. Without a refresh strategy, performance would plateau and costs would spike.

Regional Complexity

Expanding to 12 new cities meant each market needed relevant messaging — but production couldn't scale 1:1 with geography.

B2B Specificity

Restaurant owners don't respond to consumer logic. Every angle had to answer one question: "What's in it for my business?"

04 — Strategic Logic

Before Building Creatives,
We Built a System

Instead of producing campaigns reactively, I structured a quarterly experimentation pipeline — each phase with a hypothesis, a creative variable, and a performance signal to validate. Every asset had a reason to exist. Every result fed the next cycle.

Core of the acquisition funnel. High-impact single-image formats optimized for Meta and Google placements. The "High Demand + Guaranteed Delivery" angle represented ~74% of total national investment in peak periods.

Step-by-step onboarding format in static and dynamic template versions. Reduced perceived complexity of joining the platform, tested in both static and template variants.

07 — Key Strategy

Fake Refresh
Strategy

When a high-performing ad starts to fatigue, the default is to rebuild it — but that resets the platform's learning phase, spikes CPC, and loses accumulated delivery efficiency. The Fake Refresh challenged that default: lightweight visual and copy updates preserved the ad's identity and platform learning while giving the algorithm a renewed signal. Validated with pre/post data across 7 cities simultaneously.

09 — Regional

One Country,
Many Audiences

National campaigns established the base. Regional data revealed that different cities had distinct conversion triggers — which shaped creative adaptation for the 12-city expansion pack. Instagram Feed and Stories were top placements across all markets. Video led CPC efficiency in most cities.

São Paulo

Seasonal creatives drove the highest engagement. Easter "Chocolate" creative secured #1 position. Predominantly male audience, 18–34 age group. Instagram stories and feed were top placements.

Rio de Janeiro

Consumer Month campaigns had stronger traction. Seasonal relevance combined with lifestyle-oriented messaging aligned with local culture. Male audience drove the majority of clicks.

Manaus

Business value and entrepreneurship messaging outperformed. "Eupreendedorismo" video combined sweet production with Easter, driving strong results. Instagram placements were top-performing.

Brasília

One of the lowest spend-volume cities, but notable: the only market where static creatives had a lower CPC than video — a unique signal for this audience. App introduction creative highlighted zero monthly fee.

Fortaleza

National ad consistently outperformed city-specific creative. Running the national campaign proved the better strategy for this market. Video led CPC efficiency with lowest cost per engagement.

Porto Alegre

ROI-focused and business-value messaging resonated with the entrepreneurial market profile. Video led CPC and engagement with lowest cost per engagement throughout the period.

12 — Learnings

Key Learnings

The biggest wins didn't come from a single brilliant creative — they came from a system that tested fast, learned consistently, and scaled what worked.

In a B2B paid media environment, creative strategy is growth strategy. Seasonal relevance, lightweight refresh cycles, and structured messaging experimentation proved more effective than budget increases: with +6% more spend, the right creative system delivered +22% more reach.
