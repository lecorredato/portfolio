12 cities, localized messaging — production couldn't scale 1:1 with geography.

B2B Specificity

Restaurant owners need real business value hooks — not consumer-style visuals.

06 — Key Strategy

Fake Refresh
Strategy

  • What it is: Lightweight visual updates (BG swap, copy angle) on top-performing ads — no full rebuild.
  • Why it matters: Avoids resetting platform learning phase, keeps CPC low, extends delivery efficiency.
  • How it was found: Pattern identified via Smartly.io's live creative feed — simultaneous updates showed extended performance windows.

09 — Learnings

Key Learnings

  • In B2B paid media, creative strategy is growth strategy — not a production cost.
  • Test fast, scale what works — biweekly cycles beat quarterly planning.
  • National + refresh outperformed city-only executions in most markets.
  • Seasonal hooks drive disproportionate results — 23% of sign-ups from one pack.
  • AI creatives aren't experimental anymore — they delivered the lowest CPC of the account.
